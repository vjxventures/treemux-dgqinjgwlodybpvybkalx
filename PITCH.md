# QuickCal - Instant Meeting Scheduling

QuickCal is a blazingly fast, beautifully designed meeting scheduler that eliminates the back-and-forth of coordinating calendars. In just 30 seconds, anyone can create a custom booking page, set their availability, and start accepting meetings—no login, no complex setup, just a clean URL to share. Unlike overcomplicated calendar tools, QuickCal focuses on what matters: making it effortless for people to find time with you. With smart time slot generation, timezone detection, and an intuitive booking flow, it's Cal.com's simplicity rebuilt from scratch for the modern web.

Built with Next.js 16, TypeScript, and shadcn/ui for a production-grade experience that actually feels fast.
